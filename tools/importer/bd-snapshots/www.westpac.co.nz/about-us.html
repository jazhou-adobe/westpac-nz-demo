Past & future.

Our first customer came from the Otago goldfields in 1861. As our nation grew, we grew too. Now around 1.5 million people bank with us.

It’s our responsibility to help where we can. We aim to support our customers to become financially secure. Through sustainable business practices, we invest-in and care for the places we live so that our people and communities thrive and succeed.

Careers at Westpac.

At Westpac New Zealand, our purpose is taking action now to create a better future. It’s what we do, who we are and why we come to work every day. What's most important to us is understanding what a better future means to our customers and helping them get there. If you’re passionate about helping our customers, we’d love to have you on our team.
